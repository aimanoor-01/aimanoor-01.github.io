About the Founder & OwnerBhakkar, Punjab, Pakistan

Aima Noor

BS English Literature & Linguistics Student • Independent Researcher & Curator

Assalam-o-Alaikum and welcome! I am Aima Noor from Bhakkar, Punjab, Pakistan. As a student of English Literature and Linguistics, I noticed how often students carry heavy academic expectations, quiet loneliness, and the fear of past failures without a safe place to speak honestly.

I founded Unspoken & Unbroken with a dual vision: to give students a judgment-free community where lived stories of struggle and recovery can breathe, and to pair those human experiences with thoughtful, well-researched articles that explore the deeper roots of academic isolation and resilience.

Community & Educational Disclaimer

This platform is an educational, reflective, and research-oriented community space curated by Aima Noor. The stories and essays shared here are for peer understanding and academic discourse—they do not constitute professional psychological, medical, or crisis counseling. If you are experiencing severe distress, please reach out to your university counseling center or a qualified mental health professional.
